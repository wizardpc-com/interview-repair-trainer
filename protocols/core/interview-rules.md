# Core Interview Protocol

These rules govern the text-first project and research deep-dive MVP. They are independent of interviewer tone and scenario-specific content.

## Question alignment

- Ask one clear surface question at a time and freeze one primary training target before the answer starts.
- Judge alignment with the explicit question, not with an imagined interviewer intention.
- Treat brief setup or context as acceptable when the answer returns to the question.
- Consider interruption only when the answer persistently addresses a different question after enough context exists.

## Evidence requirement

- Required evidence must be reasonably demanded by the surface question and recorded before the answer starts.
- Missing required evidence may become gate-eligible only after the answer has had enough time to supply it.
- Optional evidence may improve final review but must never cause an interruption when absent.
- Do not require a precise number unless the surface question reasonably asks for measurement or quantitative support.

## Personal ownership

- Team context and “we” language are allowed.
- When the surface question explicitly asks for personal contribution, require a clear statement of the candidate's own action, decision, or responsibility.
- Do not interrupt if initial team language is followed by a clear personal contribution.

## Uncertainty and boundary awareness

- Accept honest limits on what was measured, validated, or concluded.
- Distinguish observation, inference, and uncertainty; never pressure the candidate to invent evidence.
- The Runtime evaluates answer structure and does not decide specialist scientific or engineering truth.
- “No reliable measurement was taken” is not automatically vague when it directly and honestly states the measurement boundary.

## Repair policy

- Preserve the original surface question, primary target, and required evidence during repair.
- Give one minimal cue focused on the detected structural issue, then allow a re-answer.
- Re-evaluate the re-answer against the same frozen target; do not introduce a new hidden criterion.
- The MVP permits at most one Hard Gate per question.

## When not to interrupt

- The answer is incomplete, mid-sentence, or has not accumulated sufficient context.
- A brief detour, setup, or team-level statement is followed by an aligned answer.
- Only optional evidence is absent.
- The candidate clearly identifies an honest uncertainty or measurement boundary.
- The evaluator is uncertain, low-confidence, unavailable, or produced invalid output.
- The checkpoint is stale, the state does not allow a gate, or the question already used its gate.
